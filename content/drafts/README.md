# yusufucuz.com — the first 10 posts

Personal-brand content for yusufucuz.com. **Not** tourist search-intent content, which belongs
on berlinwalk.com. Strict split so the two domains never compete for the same queries:

- **berlinwalk.com** → what visitors search for ("Berlin in 2 days", "is X worth it")
- **yusufucuz.com** → who Yusuf is: craft, opinion, field notes, career. Builds author
  authority and funnels to the private-tour form (`#book`).

English only. Every post ends with a soft CTA to `#book`.

## Hard rule for every agent that touches this folder

**Never invent a number, a review, a guest quote, a guest count, or an anecdote.**
Missing fact → leave a `[YUSUF: ...]` placeholder and stop. A post ships incomplete rather
than fabricated. See `ANSWERS.md` for everything Yusuf has actually confirmed.

Two standing constraints from him:
- **No euro figures** for tips or earnings, anywhere.
- **Nothing may imply past private-tour clients.** He has not run one yet. The private tour
  is described as an offer, never as a track record — and the fact that it is new is not
  mentioned either way.

## What changed after the interview

Four of the original ten did not survive contact with the facts:

- **Original 05 (how I plan a private tour) killed.** He has never run one. Replaced with
  *Talking to twenty people, and talking to two*, built entirely on the free tour.
- **04 went from three skips to one.** He only endorsed the Fernsehturm platform, so the piece
  became one skip plus three redirections, in a constructive tone.
- **03 lost Checkpoint Charlie.** Not on his route. The piece is now built on the fact that his
  most-asked question is about the one thing his tour does not show.
- **09 stopped being a reading list.** Only two books he has actually read survive. The piece is
  now about the old photographs and maps he carries, which is what guests praise most and what
  the website never mentions.

## Two findings from the reviews that outrank the content plan

Read from the FreeTour listing on 16 Aug 2026, all 26 reviews:

1. **Eight reviewers, unprompted, say this is not the usual Berlin tour** — everyone else does
   the Wall and the Cold War, his starts eight hundred years earlier. Guests state his
   positioning more clearly than the site does. **The homepage copy should use this.**
2. **The small tours produce the best reviews.** What he experiences as a bad morning (1–2
   people) is what guests describe as intimate and the best version of the product. This
   reframed posts 02 and 05 and is worth acting on commercially, not just editorially.

## Series (these carry on past post 10, at one a day)

- **Guest asked me** — a real question from a real tour, answered honestly
- **Field notes** — one place, one observation, ~700 words
- **Craft** — how guiding actually works
- **Opinion** — arguments about Berlin and about tourism
- **Career** — the marketing-to-guiding story, used sparingly

## Daily pipeline

1. Yusuf records a ~3 minute voice note after a tour: what was asked, what he noticed.
2. Agent drafts into the matching series, in his voice: first person, plain, no hype,
   no em dashes.
3. Fact-check pass: every claim traceable to a named source, or it is cut.
4. Nothing publishes with an unresolved `[YUSUF: ...]` placeholder.
